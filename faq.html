General Acupuncture

Acupuncture is generally painless. The needles we use are ultra-fine—much thinner than standard medical syringes. While you may feel a slight pinch upon insertion, most patients report a deep sense of relaxation and warmth during the treatment. Many even fall asleep!
The number of treatments depends on the severity and duration of your condition. Acute issues may resolve in 2 to 4 sessions, while chronic, long-term conditions might require a commitment of 8 to 12 sessions for lasting results.
We recommend wearing loose, comfortable clothing. Practitioner access to your lower arms and legs (up to the elbows and knees) is usually required. If specific areas like your back or shoulders need treatment, we will provide proper draping to ensure your comfort and modesty.
Most patients leave feeling deeply relaxed, grounded, and rejuvenated. Occasionally, some people might feel a mild soreness at the needle sites or slightly fatigued, which is a normal response as the body continues its natural healing process.
Yes! Acupuncture is incredibly safe and highly effective during pregnancy when performed by a licensed professional. It is commonly used to treat morning sickness, lower back pain, sciatica, and even to help induce labor when the time is right.
Absolutely not. We strictly adhere to Clean Needle Technique (CNT) protocols. Every single needle used is sterile, single-use, individually packaged, and safely disposed of in a medical sharps container immediately after your treatment.

Herbs & Alternative Therapies

Fire cupping uses heated glass cups to create suction on the skin, drawing out deep muscle tension and increasing blood flow. It does leave circular marks, but these are not bruises (which are caused by blunt trauma). The marks indicate stagnation being drawn to the surface and typically fade within 3 to 7 days.
Yes, when prescribed by a licensed herbalist. Our clinic only sources herbs from reputable, FDA-compliant, GMP-certified distributors that rigorously test for heavy metals, pesticides, and contaminants to ensure absolute safety and purity.
In most cases, yes. However, you must provide a full list of your current medications and supplements during your consultation. Our practitioners are highly trained in identifying herb-drug interactions and will customize a safe formula for you.
Traditional herbal teas can have strong, earthy, and sometimes bitter flavors. If you are sensitive to tastes, let us know! We can provide your custom herbal formulation in convenient capsule, pill, or easily dissolvable granule forms.
Moxibustion involves safely burning dried mugwort (moxa) near the surface of the skin over specific acupuncture points. This deeply penetrates the meridians to warm the body, expel cold and dampness, and profoundly stimulate the flow of Qi and blood.
Gua Sha is a therapeutic technique that uses a smooth-edged tool to gently scrape the skin. It is incredibly effective for breaking down tight fascial tissue, reducing acute inflammation, improving lymphatic drainage, and relieving tension in the neck and shoulders.

Policies & Billing

Yes, we are in-network with several major PPO insurance plans. Because acupuncture coverage varies widely, we ask that you provide your insurance details prior to your first visit so our billing team can verify your specific benefits and co-pays.
Absolutely. Acupuncture and Traditional Chinese Medicine consultations are generally considered eligible medical expenses. We can process your Flexible Spending Account (FSA) or Health Savings Account (HSA) cards directly at the front desk.
We require a minimum of 24 hours' notice to cancel or reschedule an appointment. Cancellations made with less than 24 hours' notice or no-shows will be subject to a cancellation fee equivalent to 50% of the scheduled service price.
Your very first visit will take approximately 90 minutes. This gives us ample time to conduct a comprehensive health history review, perform holistic diagnostic checks, and administer your first full acupuncture treatment. Standard follow-ups are 60 minutes.
Yes. Because true healing is a cumulative process, we offer discounted prepaid treatment packages for patients committing to a longer-term healing plan. Please view our Pricing page or ask our front desk for our current package rates.
To respect the time of our practitioners and other patients, your appointment must end at the scheduled time regardless of when you arrive. If you arrive more than 15 minutes late, we may need to reschedule your appointment and a late cancellation fee will apply.
