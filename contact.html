Patient Guide

Preparing for Your
First Visit.

To ensure the most accurate diagnosis and the most comfortable treatment experience, we ask that you make a few simple preparations before arriving at the clinic.

  • Eat a Light Meal Do not arrive on an empty stomach. Eating a small meal or snack 1-2 hours prior prevents dizziness.
  • Wear Loose Clothing Wear comfortable clothing that can easily be rolled up to your elbows and knees.
  • Avoid Caffeine & Alcohol These can alter your pulse and heart rate, making traditional tongue and pulse diagnosis less accurate.
