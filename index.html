Does acupuncture hurt?

Acupuncture is generally painless. The needles used are ultra-fine—much thinner than medical syringes. Most patients report feeling a slight tingle, warmth, or a deep sense of relaxation during the session. Many even fall asleep!

How many treatments will I need?

The number of treatments depends entirely on the nature, severity, and duration of your condition. Acute issues may resolve in 2 to 4 sessions, while chronic conditions might require a longer-term commitment of 8 to 12 sessions.

Do you accept health insurance?

Yes, we are in-network with several major health insurance providers. Coverage varies by plan, so we recommend contacting our front desk with your insurance details prior to your first visit so we can verify your specific acupuncture benefits.

Are Chinese herbs safe to take with medication?

Our practitioners are highly trained in herb-drug interactions. During your consultation, please provide a complete list of all medications and supplements you are currently taking so we can formulate a completely safe herbal prescription for you.
